# Kyro SEO Roadmap

Last updated: 2026-07-21

Owner: Head of SEO

Status: Single source of truth

## Executive Summary

Kyro's SEO mission is to become the most authoritative website for flexible gym access, gym day passes and staying consistent with fitness while travelling.

This roadmap replaces ad hoc keyword lists with a structured content system. It prioritises evergreen searches with real demand and clear user problems. Kyro should not chase tiny local keywords such as "gyms near Victoria Station" or "gyms near Manchester Airport" unless a future partner/supply strategy makes that page commercially necessary.

The highest-value Kyro SEO pages should answer questions a traveller, digital nomad, remote worker or business traveller would realistically search before trying to train away from home.

## Strategic Guardrails

### Build

- Evergreen informational plus commercial pages.
- Pages that explain gym access, day passes, visitor access, short-term access and training consistency while travelling.
- Pages that naturally link to published pillar pages.
- Pages that can earn links from travel, remote-work, business travel, fitness and gym-industry sites.
- Pages that can eventually convert into waitlist signups and future pass purchases.

### Avoid

- Tiny local pages without meaningful demand.
- Airport/station/neighbourhood pages unless attached to a clear evergreen travel problem.
- Generic fitness advice.
- Weight loss, bodybuilding, supplements or nutrition advice.
- Detailed workout-plan content that makes Kyro look like a coaching product rather than a gym-access platform.

## Scoring Model

Every article is scored out of 100.

| Factor | Weight | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Search demand | 15 | Likelihood that people realistically search the topic |
| Relevance to Kyro | 20 | Fit with gym access, day passes and travel fitness |
| Commercial intent | 15 | Likelihood the searcher may join the waitlist or later buy a pass |
| Evergreen value | 15 | Whether the page can stay useful for years |
| Backlink potential | 10 | Ability to earn links or citations |
| Ease of ranking | 10 | Relative difficulty for a young site |
| Topical authority value | 15 | How much the page strengthens Kyro's SEO moat |
